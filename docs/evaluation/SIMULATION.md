## Average user per month

- **Model tokens (estimate):** 55,568. Cost: set `pricing` in `backend/app/models.yaml` to see it (prices are not guessed).
- **Learned cab skill:** 38% fewer LLM steps for cab rides once the routine is active (3 rides teach it, 2 shadow rides check it, then it runs without the model).
- **Data, excluding voice:** 3.1 KB a month of API traffic for messages (measured).
- **Live voice:** ≈614 MB a month at 5 min/day, because audio travels as raw 16/24 kHz PCM in base64 JSON. It dominates bandwidth: Opus at ~16 kbit/s each way between the phone and our server (decoded there) would cut it to under a tenth.
- **Privacy:** OTP messages never reach the cloud (they are counted under "on phone").

## Hand-picked cases per profile (what personalisation should look like)

**Ramesh Sharma** (type 2 diabetes, knee arthritis)
- Book an auto to Saket market → Asks: auto or car? A car is easier on his knees; keeps the auto only if he insists.
- Shows a packet of gulab jamun in the shopping camera → Reads the label, then gently: high sugar, maybe the sugar-free one, given his diabetes.
- Mera OTP batao → Says the sentence in Gemini's voice; the six digits are spoken by the phone with the mic off.

**Sunita Verma** (high blood pressure)
- Shows salted chips in the shopping camera → Reminds her of the blood pressure check on Monday: very salty, maybe the roasted makhana.
- Mujhe doctor appointment ke liye cab chahiye → Goes to Fortis Memorial Gurugram for her BP check; no need to ask where.
- Kitne paise aaye Priya se? → Reads the credit; the amount is spoken by the phone, never sent to the cloud.

**Arjun Mehta** (blind (no useful vision))
- Book a cab to Centre for Sight → Every step spoken; the confirm reads car, fare and ETA; yes by voice, no screen needed.
- Is this message about my parcel real? → Says it's a scam (fake India Post domain) without him seeing the link.

**Kamla Devi** (arthritis, uses a wheelchair, hard of hearing)
- Hospital jaana hai cab se → Picks a car with room for the wheelchair (XL), not Go or an auto, and says why.
- Rohit ka message aaya hai, paise bhej doon? → Stops her: classic 'relative in accident' scam; offers to call her daughter first.
